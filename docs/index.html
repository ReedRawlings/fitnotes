Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in and no server. Everything is stored on your iPhone.

How do I back up or move my data? Settings → Preferences → Export Workouts creates a CSV of every set you've logged. Your data is also included in your normal iCloud or computer device backup.

Why didn't my rest timer alert me? The app asks for notification permission the first time you start a rest timer. If you declined, turn notifications on in the iOS Settings app under Progressive Lift.

Does deleting the app delete my workouts? Yes. Export first if you want to keep them.

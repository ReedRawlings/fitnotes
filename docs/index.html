Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in, and the app has no server of its own. Everything is stored on your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud.

How do I back up or move my data? Settings → Preferences → Export Workouts creates a CSV of every set you've logged. If you are signed in to iCloud, your workouts also sync to your other devices automatically.

Why didn't my rest timer alert me? The app asks for notification permission the first time you start a rest timer. If you declined, turn notifications on in the iOS Settings app under Progressive Lift.

Does deleting the app delete my workouts? It deletes them from that iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, a synced copy stays in your iCloud and comes back when you reinstall with the same Apple Account. To remove that copy too, delete Progressive Lift's data in your iCloud storage settings in the iOS Settings app. If you don't use iCloud, deleting the app deletes your workouts, so export them first if you want to keep them.

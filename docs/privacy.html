Privacy Policy

Progressive Lift · Effective November 25, 2026

Progressive Lift does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your workouts, exercises, routines, goals, and preferences are stored only on your device. They never leave it unless you choose to export them.

Exports

If you use Export Workouts, a CSV file is created and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, AirDrop, email, another app) is entirely your choice.

Notifications and Live Activities

Rest timer alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

Backups

Your data may be included in your own iCloud or computer device backups, which are controlled by your Apple settings, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: see the support page.

Support · Progressive Lift is available on the App Store for iPhone.
